Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to the Mara

Drive: about 5 hours. Stay: Mara lodge, full board.

Pick-up in Nairobi, lunch on the way and an evening game drive.

Day 2: Full day in the reserve

Morning and afternoon game drives with a picnic lunch near the Mara River.

Day 3: Plains and a Maasai village visit

Sunrise drive, optional cultural visit and a last evening on the plains.

Day 4: Return to Nairobi

Breakfast, morning drive out and drop-off at the airport or your hotel.

Questions travellers ask

When is the best time to visit the Masai Mara?
July to October is the migration season and the busiest. January to February is dry with good wildlife viewing and fewer vehicles. April and May are the wettest months and the cheapest.
Is this safari wheelchair accessible?
Yes, on request. We can provide a lift-equipped vehicle and step-free lodge rooms. Tell us your needs when you book so we can confirm them.
How do I pay and what is the deposit?
A deposit secures your dates. You can pay by M-Pesa, card or bank transfer, and the balance is due before you travel.
Can I change the dates after booking?
Yes. Changes are free up to 30 days before you travel, subject to lodge availability.
